# Privacy and cookies

This site sets **no cookie**, runs **no analytics** and loads **no third party font or script**.

## What your browser stores

- Your color scheme choice (light, dark or system), in `localStorage` (`__palette`), so the next page keeps it.
- The star and fork counts of the repository shown in the header, in `sessionStorage` (`__source`), so they are fetched once per visit.

Neither leaves your browser.

## Who receives your requests

- **GitLab Pages** (GitLab Inc., United States) serves every page and, like any web server, receives your IP address and browser details with each request.
- **The GitHub API** (`api.github.com`, GitHub Inc.) answers the request your browser sends for the repository counts in the header, and receives your IP address with it.

See the [GitLab privacy statement](https://about.gitlab.com/privacy/) and the
[GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Changes

If an analytics or embedded service is ever added, this page will say which one, what it collects, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
